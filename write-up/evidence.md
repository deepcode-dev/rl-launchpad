# Evidence

Final evidence is generated from `NEW_checkpoints/ppo_v2/ppo_v2_eval_summary.json`, the five per-seed custom evaluation JSON files, the five pulled Slurm training logs, `baselines/brax_go1_200m_eval_summary.json`, and the post-hoc EMA ablation `baselines/brax_go1_200m_ema_ablation.json`. `eval/validate_submission.py` checks the fixed 20,000–20,049 episode block, 50 episodes per seed, deterministic actions, step budgets, observation/action dimensions, and measured Brax checkpoint curves. The final benchmark is five custom seeds versus three Brax seeds. The custom curve is a measured mean ± seed standard deviation over 244 logged points per seed.
